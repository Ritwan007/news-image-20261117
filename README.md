# Tuesday 17 November 2026 — The World in One Day

Entry for the make.fun competition `news-image-20261117`: one image that sums
up the world news of Tuesday 17 November 2026.

## The image

A newspaper front page, 1024×1024, with six stories in order of consequence,
plus a footer strip with two more:

1. **Kerch Bridge hit again** — Ukraine struck the bridge a second day; the
   road span was reported collapsed in unverified video. Kyiv's bridges were
   also hit.
2. **Tankers hit at Hormuz** — mines struck tankers in the Strait of Hormuz;
   Iran claims four were hit (unverified). A third US carrier arrived in the
   region.
3. **Trump in Manila** — first Quad summit in two years; Xi–Putin talks set
   for Thursday.
4. **Nvidia soars +103%** — $115.8bn revenue, first earnings since Jensen
   Huang resigned; the board says it will not reinstate him.
5. **US loses measles-free status** — elimination status lost after 26 years;
   5,412 cases in 2026.
6. **EU–Rwanda return hub** — five states plan a deportation hub for
   rejected asylum seekers. Announced Monday 16 Nov; pilot of ~100 people,
   from 2027.

Footer: Russian An-12 crash kills 19; UK sanctions 8 settlement firms and
the aid-blocking group Tzav 9.

Every label was checked against primary reporting (Reuters, AP, BBC, CDC,
PAHO). Claims that could not be verified are labelled as reported or
unverified, never as confirmed fact, and the Rwanda hub is dated to its
Monday announcement.

## The program

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) copies `entry.png` to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`. The output is byte-identical on every run.

Run it locally:

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
